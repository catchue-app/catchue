Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

The information used depends on whether you use sharing or only capture and edit on your device. 日本語

1. Camera and on-device editing

Color detection, focusing, and sticker cutouts are processed on your device. The camera feed is not continuously sent to a server. Regular captures and manual collages are saved to Photos. Supported captures are also saved as drafts for that day. Drafts are shared only when you post them. Images prepared for posting exclude EXIF data such as location and camera details. The originals in Photos are unchanged.

2. Sign in with Apple

We verify your account using the app-specific identifier and credentials issued by Apple. We do not request your Apple name or email address. Sharing uses your nickname, avatar, friend code, friendships, blocks, room memberships and invitations, profile colors and photos, and sharing settings.

3. Who can see Today and Rooms posts

We process submitted images, capture dates, assigned colors, and reactions. Regular Today posts are shared with mutual friends. Posting a regular photo lets you see your friends’ photos for the day. Room members can see each other’s photos after shooting ends. Profile colors and photos are visible to you and your friends. There is no public feed.

4. PURI captures and collaborative editing

PURI uses participant details, invitations, captured photos, votes, drawings, stickers, and finished images to support collaborative editing and deliver the result to participants. Session content is shared with participants. If the host chooses to post the work to Today, Today’s audience rules apply. The finished work is saved in each participant’s Works. Server copies used for capture, editing, and delivery are cleaned up after delivery and receipt are confirmed.

5. Stickers and Gifts

Where Gifts is available, we process the sticker image, sender, eligible recipients, expiry, and claim status. Unclaimed gifts become unavailable when they expire or are withdrawn. Claimed stickers remain usable by the recipient until removed. A Today collage may include a gift you were eligible for at the cutoff that is still available when the collage is generated. The sticker stays in the finished work even if the gift is later withdrawn. Automatic placement does not claim a gift in Gifts.

6. Photo and artwork retention

Daily post images are deleted through artwork generation and expiry processing. Room post images are deleted after the room expires. Server copies of automatic collages are temporary and are removed after receipt or the collection deadline. Received works are saved in Works on your device. Profile photos (up to six) are kept for sharing until you remove them or delete your account.

7. iCloud backup

When enabled, received artwork is backed up to your private iCloud storage. Where supported, custom stickers sync between devices using the same Catchue account. Turning it off stops new backups and sync, and existing backups remain. Your Sign in with Apple account and your device’s iCloud account are separate.

8. Notifications and safety records

If you allow notifications, your push token and preferences are used for featured colors, artwork, invitations, reactions, and similar alerts. We process reports, moderation records, usage limits, and identifiers derived from connection information to prevent misuse. You can change notification, Photos, and camera permissions in iOS Settings.

9. Withdrawing posts and deleting your account

Withdrawing a post stops its display. Removal from affected collages is applied after devices come online. Use Delete Account in Profile to request deletion of your account, friendships, posts, profile information, and your saved works and backups. We also process revocation of Sign in with Apple. Collaboratively made PURI artwork and claimed gifts may remain with other participants or recipients. Photo IDs and timestamps needed to propagate removals are kept for the configured period (up to five years by default). We cannot recall exported images or screenshots. Deleting the app does not delete your account.

10. Service providers

We use Cloudflare for the sharing API, database, and image storage, and Apple for sign-in, notifications, and iCloud. If you buy or restore something through StoreKit, transaction details such as product IDs are used to verify access. Where automatic moderation is enabled, AWS Rekognition evaluates posted images, profile photos, and thumbnails.

11. Device settings, widgets, and videos

Selected colors and settings are saved on your device. Supported widgets use up to 12 reduced-size photos from Today that you can view, along with color, room, and artwork status, through on-device sharing. A limited read-only credential fetches updates. The regular sign-in token is not shared with the widget. Recap videos are made on the device from saved artwork, and Profile lets you clear their cache. We do not use ads or third-party tracking analytics.

12. Changes and contact

Changes will be posted here. For questions about how your information is handled, contact catchue.app@gmail.com.
