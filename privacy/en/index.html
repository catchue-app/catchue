Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

This is how CatcHue (“the app”) handles your information. 日本語

1. Information we collect

The app does not collect any personal information, and does not send any information to the developer’s servers.

2. Camera

The camera feed is used only on your device, to find your chosen color and focus on it. It is never stored or sent anywhere.

3. Photos

Your photos and collages are saved to albums for each color in the Photos app on your device. The app reads photos only to show them in its albums and collages.

4. Settings and records

Your chosen color, settings and shooting records (such as the number of photos per day) are stored only on your device, and shared between the app and its widget to show them on your Home Screen.

5. Notifications

The notification at the end of a hunt is scheduled on your device. No information is sent anywhere.

6. Sharing

Photos and collages are shared only when you tap a share button. Shared content is handled according to the policy of the service you share to. Invite links contain only the hunt color, end time and goal.

7. Ads and analytics

The app has no ads, and uses no analytics or tracking tools. This website does not use analytics either.

8. Children

The app can be used by people of any age. Since it collects no information, it collects no information from children.

9. Changes

Any changes to this policy will be posted on this page.
