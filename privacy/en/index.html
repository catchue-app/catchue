Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

The information used depends on whether you use sharing or only capture and edit on your device. 日本語

1. Camera and photos

Color detection, reticle tracking and tap-to-focus run on your device. The app does not continuously send the camera feed to a server. Captured photos and manual collages are saved to Photos. Turning Post on does not share a photo until you choose to submit it.

2. Sign in with Apple

Sharing uses the app-specific identifier and authentication credentials issued by Apple to verify your account. We do not request your Apple name or email address. Sharing also uses your nickname, selected avatar, friend code, friendships, blocks, room memberships and invitations, and submitted photos with their shooting day and color, and photo reactions.

3. Who can see photos

Regular posts are visible to mutual friends. Room posts are visible to members of that room. Posting a regular photo that day unlocks your friends’ posts for that day. In timed rooms, other members’ photos are revealed after shooting ends. Photo reactions are visible to the author of the photo. There is no public worldwide feed.

4. Photo and artwork storage

Submitted images have location and camera EXIF metadata removed. Your originals in Photos are unchanged. Daily posts are deleted after the shooting day through artwork generation and expiry processing. Room posts are deleted after the room expires. Server copies of collages are temporary and are deleted after receipt or their collection deadline. Received collages are stored in Works on your device.

5. iCloud backup

When enabled, received collages are backed up to your private iCloud database. Turning backup off stops new backups and keeps previously saved artwork. Your Sign in with Apple account and your device’s iCloud account are separate. Recovery may not be possible if you delete the app before syncing, disable iCloud or run out of iCloud storage.

6. Notifications and safety records

If you allow notifications, we use your device’s push token and preferences for colors, collages, invitations and reactions. We keep reports, moderation review records, and usage counters or storage totals to prevent abuse. Identifiers derived from connection information are used to limit excessive sign-in requests. Photos are not used for advertising or user tracking.

7. Removing posts and deleting your account

Removing a post stops its display and propagates removal of its photo from received collages when devices go online. Delete your account from Profile to remove account information, friendships, posts, uncollected collages, and your stored artwork and backups, and process revocation of Apple authorization. Deleting the app alone does not delete your account. Records needed to propagate deletion, such as photo IDs and timestamps, remain for the configured retention period (up to five years by default). Exported images, screenshots and immediate removal from offline devices cannot be recalled.

8. Service providers

Sharing uses Cloudflare for the API, database and temporary image storage, and Apple for sign-in, notifications and iCloud. In environments where automatic image moderation is enabled, submitted images and thumbnails are sent to AWS Rekognition for moderation. Connection information handled by these providers is also subject to their own policies.

9. Settings, widgets and advertising

Selected colors and app settings are stored on your device. The app shares display information about colors, rooms and artwork availability with its widgets; it does not share authentication tokens or submitted images with them. The app uses no advertising or third-party user-tracking analytics.

10. Changes and contact

Changes to this policy will be reflected here. Contact: catchue.app@gmail.com.
